damage_notifier
/datum/component/damage_notifier

Damage Notifier Component

Reports every damage and healing change on a tagged carbon to the admins that tagged it: damage type, amount, bodypart or organ, resulting health and the proc/object/user that caused it.

Sources are the /datum/admins holders watching the parent. Added by /datum/admins/proc/add_tagged_datum, removed by /datum/admins/proc/remove_tagged_datum; the component deletes itself when the last watcher leaves.
